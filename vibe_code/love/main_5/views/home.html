Welcome to WhatsApp Messenger

Connect with friends and family around the world for free. WhatsApp offers simple, secure, reliable messaging and calling, available on phones all over the world.

Simple and Secure

Send messages and photos, share updates, and make video calls – all for free.* WhatsApp is available on phone and desktop.

End-to-End Encryption

Some of your most personal moments are shared on WhatsApp, which is why we built end-to-end encryption into the latest versions of our app. When end-to-end encrypted, your messages and calls are secured so only you and the person you're communicating with can read or listen to them, and nobody in between, not even WhatsApp.

Voice and Video Calls

Talk to your friends and family for free*. Even over different countries. With voice and video calls, you can have face-to-face conversations when voice or text isn't enough.

Our Story

Bloom & Petal was founded in 2015 by floral designer Jane Doe, who turned a lifelong passion for gardening and artistry into a beloved local flower shop. It all began in a small studio, with a commitment to sourcing the freshest, most beautiful seasonal flowers from local growers. We believe that every bouquet tells a story, and we pour our hearts into crafting unique arrangements that capture the sentiment of our customers.


Our philosophy is simple: celebrate nature's beauty. We prioritize sustainable practices, from composting our green waste to using eco-friendly packaging. We've grown from a one-woman show to a small team of dedicated florists who share the same vision. We are more than just a flower shop; we are a community of plant lovers, artists, and storytellers. Thank you for being a part of our journey.

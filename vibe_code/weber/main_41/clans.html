Clans in Clash of Clans

Clans are the heart of Clash of Clans, allowing players to team up, support each other, and compete in epic Clan Wars. Joining a clan is essential for a full game experience!

Why Join a Clan?

🤝

Troop Sharing

Request and donate troops to your clanmates, providing crucial reinforcements for defense and attack.

⚔️

Clan Wars

Compete against other clans in strategic battles, earning war loot and bragging rights.

🎮

Clan Games

Work together with your clan to complete challenges and unlock valuable rewards for everyone.

💬

Community & Support

Chat with fellow players, share strategies, and get advice to improve your game.

Types of Clans

Farming Clans ▶

These clans focus on resource gathering. Members often leave their Town Hall outside their base to get a free shield, protecting their resources while they're offline. They might not participate in wars as frequently.

War Clans ▶

Dedicated to Clan Wars, these clans prioritize battle strategies, troop donations for war, and strong attack performance. They often have strict rules for war participation and attack planning.

Hybrid Clans ▶

Hybrid clans balance farming and warring. They participate in wars regularly but also allow members to focus on upgrading their bases and collecting resources. They offer a more flexible environment.

Social/Casual Clans ▶

These clans are primarily for players who want to chat, make friends, and enjoy the game without intense pressure. Wars and farming might be secondary, with a focus on a relaxed atmosphere.

Clan Ranks & Roles

Within a clan, members hold different ranks, each with specific responsibilities:

  • Member: Standard clan member. Can request and donate troops.
  • Elder: Can invite and kick members (with a cooldown).
  • Co-Leader: Has significant power, can start wars, promote/demote, and accept/reject join requests.
  • Leader: The founder of the clan, with full control over all clan settings and members.

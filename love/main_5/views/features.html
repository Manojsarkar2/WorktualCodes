WhatsApp Features

Voice and Video Calls

Make crystal-clear voice and video calls to friends and family around the world. Enjoy high-quality connections even on slower networks.

End-to-End Encryption

Your personal messages and calls are secured with end-to-end encryption, ensuring that only you and the person you're communicating with can read or listen to them.

Groups

Create groups to easily share messages, photos, and videos with up to 256 people. Stay connected with your family, friends, and colleagues.

WhatsApp Web and Desktop

Use WhatsApp on your computer for seamless communication across all your devices. Stay in sync and never miss a message.

Status Updates

Share text, photo, video, and GIF updates that disappear after 24 hours. Let your friends know what you're up to throughout the day.

Document Sharing

Easily send and receive documents, PDFs, spreadsheets, and more. Share important files with your contacts directly within WhatsApp.
